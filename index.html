How to keep this

A few simple ways to come back to your Queens and keep what you write.

  1. Save the link. Bookmark this page or add it to your phone's home screen so you can return to it whenever you need a Queen's guidance.
  2. Your journal saves itself. When you write with a Queen and tap "Save to journal," your words stay in this browser on this device, ready for next time.
  3. Keep a copy you own. Open "My journal" and tap "Copy my journal," then paste it into your notes app, GoodNotes, or a document.
  4. Save a card. After you draw, take a screenshot of the card. It makes a lovely phone wallpaper or a reminder for your altar.

Your entries live in this browser only. They won't follow you to another phone or browser, and clearing your browser data will erase them, so copy anything you want to keep.
